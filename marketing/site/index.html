What's inside

A planetarium for the real world.

Built on a custom Metal renderer with HDR bloom, a physically inspired atmosphere and the live aurora oval. Every dot is real data.

Ride with the ISS

Chase the space station 400 km up at 28,000 km/h, over city lights, aurora and green airglow, with a countdown to the next orbital sunrise.

Rewind the planet

Replay the last 24 hours in half a minute: daylight sweeps round the globe as every earthquake ripples in where it struck.

Dive into any city

Zoom in and NASA imagery streams in at 500 m: mountains in shaded relief by day, street grids of light by night.

Planet Briefing

An AI-written documentary of what's happening right now, flown in 3D with word-synced captions and a generative soundscape.

Pulse

Earthquakes, storms, wildfires and volcanoes as they happen, with depth cross-sections, energy in TNT and a haptic "feel it".

Space Weather & Aurora

The Sun minutes ago from GOES-19, solar wind, flares, the Kp index and your own odds of seeing the aurora tonight.

Tonight's Sky

Visible ISS and Tiangong passes with a sky-dome path, the Moon's phase and twilight, all computed on your iPhone.

Ask Kármán

A planetary scientist that answers with live data: why that quake happened, where the storm is heading, whether you'll see the aurora.

Widgets, Live Activities, Siri

Earth Now, Aurora and Space Station widgets, launch countdowns in the Dynamic Island, and "Play the planet briefing in Kármán" with Siri.
